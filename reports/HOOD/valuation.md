# HOOD — Robinhood Markets, Inc. — Valuation Report

**Date:** 2026-04-20  **Current Price:** $89.85  **Market Cap:** ~$81B  **Shares Out:** 790M

---

## 1. Company Overview

Robinhood Markets operates a commission-free retail brokerage and financial-services platform covering equities, options, crypto, futures, margin lending, retirement accounts, a Gold subscription, and a consumer credit card. The business monetizes primarily through (a) payment for order flow and transaction-based revenues, (b) net interest on customer cash, margin, and securities-lending balances, and (c) subscription and other service revenues. After years of post-IPO losses (FY21 net loss of $3.7B), HOOD inflected to durable GAAP profitability in 2024 and posted $1.88B net income on $4.47B total net revenues in 2025. The business is capital-light, benefits from rising interest-rate balances, and is levered to retail trading activity and crypto cycles.

## 11. Valuation Summary

Rationale: capex/D&A is 0.63x (capital-light, favoring EPS), *but* SBC is a material 6.8% of revenue (favoring Revenue DCF which treats SBC as cash cost), and the 2024–25 EPS is distorted by tax-valuation-allowance reversal. Revenue DCF more cleanly captures cash-economics per share.

## 12. Verdict

### OVERVALUED  (>25% downside to probability-weighted fair value)

**Explanation.** HOOD is an excellent, high-ROIC, capital-light franchise riding strong tailwinds — revenue +52% in 2025 on top of +58% in 2024, $4B of net cash, 32% ROIC, and optionality in crypto, tokenization, retirement, and credit. None of that is in dispute. The dispute is price. The reverse DCF shows the market is pricing in ~30% starting EPS growth fading to 3% — effectively the **bull case** as the **expected** case. With a 2.46 beta, a partially tax-boosted 2025 EPS, and genuine cyclical risk in crypto/retail-trading volumes, assigning 50%+ probability to the bull case is not warranted. The probability-weighted fair value of ~$47 is roughly half the market price.

**Upside risks (why I could be wrong):**
1. Crypto/tokenization revenue scales faster than modeled — the 2025 print already reflects a crypto-friendly environment, and regulatory tailwinds for on-chain securities could multiply the ARPU base.
2. Retirement IRA/401(k) rollover product gains share: unlike trading, retirement flows are sticky, grow with balances, and command high valuation multiples.
3. HOOD's $4B net-cash war chest funds accretive M&A at depressed fintech prices (Bitstamp, TradePMR, WonderFi acquisitions already landed in 2024–25).

**Downside risks:**
1. Tax-rate normalization from 10.7% (2025) toward 21–25% statutory compresses near-term EPS growth below the 22% base assumption — 2026 EPS could be flat-to-down even on strong revenue.
2. A crypto bear market or retail-risk-off rotation would directly impair transaction revenue; net interest revenues would also compress if the Fed cuts materially.
3. SBC of ~$305M/yr, buybacks of ~$650M/yr — a sizable chunk of FCFF is spent just to offset dilution rather than compound equity. If growth stalls, the cash return story unravels.

**Conviction: Medium.** The valuation gap is large and directionally clear, but HOOD's high beta (2.46) and genuine business momentum create wide error bars. A bull-case outcome is plausible; what is not plausible is paying bull-case price for a probability-weighted outcome. I would prefer to own HOOD in the $40–50 range.
