---

## 1. Company Overview

Alphabet is the world's dominant digital advertising platform through Google Search, YouTube, and its ad network, commanding ~30% of global digital ad spend. The company also operates the third-largest cloud provider (Google Cloud), which reached profitability in 2023 and is growing at 30%+. Revenue grew from $90B (2016) to $403B (2025), an 18% CAGR, with operating margins expanding to 32%. Alphabet is in the midst of a massive AI infrastructure build-out ($91B capex in 2025, 4.3x depreciation), positioning for AI-driven monetization across Search, Cloud, and new products. With a 30%+ ROIC far exceeding its 8.7% cost of equity, every dollar of growth creates substantial value.

## 12. Verdict: FAIRLY VALUED

Alphabet trades within 10% of our probability-weighted fair value of $311, placing it squarely in fairly valued territory. The market prices in ~11% EPS growth, which is reasonable for a $400B+ revenue company — our base case of 14% is modestly more optimistic, supported by AI-enhanced Search monetization and Cloud's profitable scaling. The 30%+ ROIC means growth creates real value, but at $3.4T market cap, the stock already reflects much of this franchise quality. The $91B capex program is the key swing factor: if AI infrastructure investments translate to durable competitive advantages and new revenue streams, the bull case ($433) is within reach; if returns disappoint, the bear case ($208) represents meaningful downside.

**Upside Risks:**
1. AI Overviews and Gemini integration drive higher Search ad monetization (RPQ acceleration) beyond the base case
2. Google Cloud accelerates to $100B+ revenue as enterprise AI workloads shift to GCP, expanding margins to 30%+
3. Waymo begins generating material revenue as autonomous ride-hailing scales in 10+ cities, unlocking a new TAM

**Downside Risks:**
1. DOJ antitrust remedies force structural changes to Search distribution (default engine agreements with Apple, Samsung), reducing Search market share by 5-10 points
2. AI-native competitors (OpenAI/ChatGPT, Perplexity) capture meaningful share of information queries, eroding the Search moat
3. The $91B+ annual capex program yields subpar returns, depressing ROIC toward cost of capital and making the stock look permanently expensive on cash flow

**Conviction: Medium.** The analysis is well-supported by both methods and peer comparables, but the narrow margin of safety (+9.4%) offers limited room for error. At the current price, Alphabet is a hold — a pullback to $240-260 (base case Revenue DCF territory) would create a more compelling entry point.
