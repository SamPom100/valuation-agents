---

## 1. Company Overview

Costco Wholesale is the world's third-largest retailer and the leading membership warehouse club operator, with over 900 locations globally. The company's competitive moat rests on a simple but powerful model: price goods at near-cost to drive membership loyalty (renewal rate ~93%), then earn virtually all profit from membership fees. This creates a self-reinforcing flywheel — massive purchasing power enables lower prices, which drives member growth, which enables even more purchasing power. Costco generated $275B in revenue in FY 2025 with a deliberately thin operating margin of 3.8%.

**Method Weighting: 60% EPS DCF / 40% Revenue DCF**

## 12. Verdict: OVERVALUED

**Fair Value: ~$642 | Current Price: $983 | Downside: ~35%**

Costco is one of the highest-quality retailers in the world, with a durable competitive moat, exceptional capital allocation, and a 40%+ ROIC that towers over its cost of capital. However, the market has fully priced in this quality — and then some. At 51x trailing earnings, Costco trades at a valuation that requires ~20% starting EPS growth (per the reverse DCF), well above its historical 14.6% CAGR and meaningfully above even the bull case assumption. Even in the most optimistic scenario (15% EPS growth, 45x terminal P/E), the stock barely reaches its current price. The sensitivity table confirms there is virtually no margin of safety — you need the top-right corner of the grid to justify $983.

This is not a call on Costco's business quality, which is exceptional. It is a call on price. A great company at the wrong price is still a poor investment.

**Upside Risks:**
1. **Membership fee increase:** Costco raised fees in Sep 2024 (+$5 to $65/$130). Another increase within the projection period would boost earnings with no incremental cost
2. **International acceleration:** China and European markets are underpenetrated, with new warehouse openings potentially exceeding historical pace
3. **E-commerce inflection:** Costco's online business is small relative to total revenue; meaningful digital scaling could surprise

**Downside Risks:**
1. **Multiple compression:** A shift in market sentiment toward value/yield could compress Costco's 50x+ P/E toward the peer median of 30x — a 40% decline on multiple alone
2. **Membership saturation:** With 136M+ cardholders, domestic membership growth may slow; incremental growth requires international markets with lower unit economics
3. **Wage/cost inflation:** As the largest private sector employer of union labor among retailers, minimum wage increases and benefits costs could compress the already-thin operating margin

**Conviction: High** — The overvaluation conclusion is robust. Even generous assumptions produce values well below the current price, and the reverse DCF confirms the market is pricing in growth that exceeds Costco's historical track record.
